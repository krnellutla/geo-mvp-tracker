# Validation Log

**Hypothesis under test:** B2B SaaS and fintech marketing leads in Singapore and SEA cannot report their brand's presence in AI answers, and would pay S$300/month for a trusted, repeatable audit.

## Part 1. Friends survey (screener, 10 multiple-choice questions)

**Purpose:** find out which friends and contacts are the right subject and who to interview next. This is a screener. It does not validate willingness to pay: friends answer politely, and a stated price band is weaker evidence than a signed re-run. Q10 is a signal, not proof. The form was revised on 10 Oct 2026: Q4, Q5, Q6, Q8 and Q10 now read as shown below, and Q10 asks about the last tool bought instead of a price band.

**Rule for reading results:** only respondents who answered Q1 "marketing, growth or SEO" or "founder/owner" and Q2 "B2B software or fintech" count towards the subject. Everyone else is logged as a segment signal (Golden Pairs Pair 5 and 7), not toward the 3-of-5 bar.

**Link:** https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeHynCvC3omxm3OiiNl9XecNaRBJHx8-B_KXQqE-kTPvC02rQ/viewform (Google Form; responses collect in the linked "AI search survey: responses" sheet)

**Scoring:** a respondent is a *strong lead* when Q1 is A or B, Q2 is A, Q4 is A or B, and Q10 is B (approved a S$100 to S$500 a month tool without procurement). Strong leads get a 20-minute Mom Test interview (Part 2).
